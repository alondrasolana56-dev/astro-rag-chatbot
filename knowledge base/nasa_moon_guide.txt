NASA Artemis - The Next Frontier (2026 Space Apps Focus)

1. LUNAR SOUTH POLE:
Water ice exists in permanently shadowed craters. Key for drinking water, oxygen, rocket fuel. LRO data confirms deposits.

2. SURVIVAL CHALLENGES:
- 14 Earth days sunlight, then 14 days darkness = need batteries + nuclear backup
- Communication delay ~1.3 seconds, needs relay satellites
- Lunar dust is sharp, abrasive, damages suits and equipment
- Radiation protection needed for habitats

- Moon Mineralogy Mapper data
